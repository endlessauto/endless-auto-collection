PPF FAQ

Front-end or full body for I-880?

Front-end covers the chip zone: bumper, hood, fenders, mirrors, headlights. Full body covers painted surfaces around the car. Commute miles on 880 usually start at front-end; full body is for owners who want the same standard everywhere.

PPF first, or ceramic first?

Film first on the panels you want protected from chips. Ceramic coats the film and the rest of the paint so water beads. Most protection packages stack both after consult.

How long does PPF last?

Packages use self-healing film with a 10-year manufacturer warranty. Real wear depends on wash habits and freeway grit. We inspect at handoff and keep the relationship after you leave.

Do you document the install?

Yes. Professional BTS photo and video of the process is part of the job — not an add-on brochure.
