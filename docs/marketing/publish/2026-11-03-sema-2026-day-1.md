- Big-show day-1 search is owned by SEMA, brands, and established car channels. We will not win "sema 2026 day 1" on search alone.

- Title formula that wins: keyword + one specific car, person, or moment. Reveal or stake beats a general recap.
- Hashtags on competitors: #SEMA plus the brand. Short and two or three at most in the title. Ours go in the description.

- Toyo's official spelling is "Treadpass" (one word). Press and SEMA coverage use it. Viewers also type "tread pass", so use both: the one-word form in the title and tags, the two-word form once in the description and as a tag.
