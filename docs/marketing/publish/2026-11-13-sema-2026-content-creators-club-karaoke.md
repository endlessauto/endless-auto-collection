## Read this first: what this video is for

This is community content, not lead content. The research and the channel audit agree on three things:

- Nobody searches for this event. "sema content creators club", "sema afterparty", and "sema after hours" return no YouTube autocomplete suggestions. The video will not be found by search.
- Generic meet and event vlogs lag on this channel. Cars and Coffee got 89 views, the Peaky Blinders party 109. Named people and named cars earn views.
- Reach will come from browse, the Short, and the people on camera sharing it. Plan the first 24 hours around that, not around SEO.

So the package leans on a concrete stake (karaoke, then ramen), real names once you have them, and a thumbnail test plan. Judge it against the channel's own baseline (148 median long-form views), not against SEMA channels.

## Music and Content ID

Karaoke audio is copyrighted music. Expect Content ID claims. Do this before going public:

1. Upload as unlisted and wait for the copyright check to finish (often a few minutes, sometimes longer).
2. If a song is claimed, trim it, mute it, or replace it with a licensed track. Do not rely on the claim being harmless.
3. Keep the sung clips short and put commentary, reactions or on-screen text over them.
4. Use the same caution on the Short and the Instagram cut.
