## Fact check: "Lamborghini Murcielago LP650 2008"

Do not publish "LP650" or "2008" until the car's badge and paperwork confirm them. The combination does not hold up against public sources.

- The **LP 640** ran 2006 to 2010. Wikipedia lists 471 kW (640 PS, 632 hp). Other spec sites list 631 to 640 hp. A 2008 car fits this model.
- The **LP 650-4 Roadster** was a 50-unit special edition with an uprated 6.5 L V12 at about 650 PS (Lamborghini's announcement: 650 hp, 478 kW), permanent four-wheel drive. It was a **Roadster only** and is dated to **2009** in one source. Sources disagree on exact horsepower, so use the owner's paperwork, not a number from the web.
- So a 2008 car is most likely an **LP640**, coupe or roadster. A real LP 650-4 would be a 2009 Roadster, which is rarer and a different story.

How to confirm in two minutes: check the title or registration for model year, check the VIN (10th character is the model year), look at the rear badge (LP 640 vs LP 650-4), and check whether the car is a coupe or roadster. Ask Arun which one he has. If it is the LP 650-4, say so loudly, because it is a selling point.

Until confirmed, the package uses "Lamborghini Murcielago" everywhere public. The generic name is also the stronger search term (see research).

Sources: [Wikipedia: Lamborghini Murciélago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lamborghini_Murci%C3%A9lago), [Lambocars: LP640 Roadster specs](https://www.lambocars.com/murcielago-lp640-roadster-specs-performance/), [Lambocars: LP 650-4 Roadster announcement](https://www.lambocars.com/?p=23637), [Italpassion: Murcielago](https://www.italpassion.fr/en/cars/lamborghini-murcielago).
