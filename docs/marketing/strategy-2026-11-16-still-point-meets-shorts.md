## 3. Content strategy to drive leads

Rule from the playbook: metric is leads per video. Every piece ends with one next step.

| Pillar | Short-form (3 per week) | Long-form (1 per week) | Lead step |
|--------|-------------------------|------------------------|-----------|
| Education | "PPF vs ceramic vs wrap in 30 seconds", "what a $4,500 vs $8,000 PPF quote means", "3 signs your tint is failing", "can PPF cover a rock chip" | How-to with a named car and job (best performer: headlights 654) | Comment a keyword, get a checklist by DM |
| Process & Craft | Satisfying one-take: foam, correction pass, PPF squeegee, wrap removal | Full install on one car, time-stamped | Book an estimate link |
| Build Stories | Named car, one stake, one question hook | What Drives You episodes | Follow the build, see the next step |
| Shop & Team | Will explains one thing in 20 seconds. Skits, sparingly | Monthly meet recap | Meet RSVP |

New formats to test, one per week for four weeks, then keep what clears the median:

1. **Will, one question, 20 seconds.** Direct to camera. "Is PPF worth it on a leased car?" Trust through a face.
2. **Myth vs fact.** Two-beat skit: customer says the myth, Will answers. Educational and fun.
3. **Price reveal.** "What this wrap removal costs and why." Transparency is the gap.
4. **Cost of skipping it.** Rock-chipped hood vs PPF'd hood, same car type.
5. **Track-day prep.** Shop-to-track pipeline once Still Point details land.

Automation next steps (build in this order):

1. **Shorts script engine** in `content-engine.md`: input a topic, output hook (text, visual, spoken), 20 to 40 second script, caption, lead keyword. Uses the Kallaway hook rule.
2. **Daily scheduled run adds one Shorts script batch** next to the carousels, drawn from the idea inbox.
3. **Lead log** (`docs/marketing/lead-log.md`): each inbound lead tagged with source video. Without it, "leads per video" stays unmeasurable.
4. **Studio paste routine**: weekly paste of CTR and average view duration so `performance.md` can act. Still the biggest open gap from the audit.
5. **Event playbook** in the skill so each meet produces a checklist, run-of-show, content shot list, and recap automatically.

## 4. AMG GT R: one-week plan (rental ends about 2026-11-23)

Existing: a red AMG GT R detail Short at 1,258 views. Titles currently carry a "mercedesbenzclkgtr" tag; the car is an AMG GT R, so fix the hashtag on future posts.

Shoot list for the week, ordered by value:

| # | Asset | Format | Hook (text = visual = spoken) | Lead step |
|---|-------|--------|-------------------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Will walks around the car and says why a track car needs PPF on the nose and mirrors | Short, 30 s | "Why a track car gets PPF first" | PPF estimate |
| 2 | Pre-track check: Will does alignment and brake look before a Still Point session | Short, 30 s | "What we check before this car sees a track" | Mechanical booking |
| 3 | Rental explainer: private one-on-one, track delivery and instruction included, intro sessions from $349 (Thunderhill), confirm prices first | Short, 25 s | "You can drive this at the track. Here's how." | stillpointauto.com or @stillpointauto |
| 4 | Detail after a session: tires, brake dust, rubber | Short, 20 s, satisfying | "This is what a track day does to a car" | Detail booking |
| 5 | Drive day vlog with a named customer or friend | Long-form, 8 to 12 min | Outcome-first title | Both brands |
| 6 | Sound and exit shot, no talking | Short, 10 s | Car name only | Follow |

Known from stillpointauto.com: the GT R is track only (the M2 is the drift car), one driver per booking, event insurance not included. Still needed from Will or Stillpoint: license and insurance requirements, session dates, revenue and credit split, and what they want said. Confirm site prices before quoting.

### On-camera script for Will (read as prompts, not lines)

Film each in one or two takes, camera at chest height, car behind. Ask Will the question; he answers in his words; we cut to the best 20 seconds.

1. "What's the first thing you protect on a car that goes to the track?" (answer: nose, mirrors, rocker panels, why)
2. "What do you check before any car goes on track?" (alignment, tire wear, brake pad and fluid, fasteners)
3. "Who is this car for, and who is it not for?" (honest answer builds trust)
4. "What does a track day actually do to paint and tires?" (rock chips, rubber pickup, brake dust)
5. "What can people bring you after a track weekend?" (detail, repair, alignment reset)
6. "Why did EAC and Still Point partner?" (one sentence, plain)
7. "If someone's never done a track day, what should they know?" (one thing, not five)

Close every take with the same line so the cut is easy: "Endless Auto Collection, Fremont. Driven by detail."

Interview tips: ask follow-up "why" once, ask for the number or the specific, re-take any answer with a filler opener. Keep the camera rolling after the answer for a clean B-roll reaction.

## 5. Monthly car meet: first pass

All numbers are `TBD` until the venue is confirmed. These are starting questions and defaults, not facts.

**Decisions needed first**

| Question | Default to propose |
|----------|-------------------|
| Where | Shop lot vs a nearby lot, decided by capacity and whether street access needs a permit |
| When | Sunday 8 to 11 am, monthly, same slot every month so people can plan |
| Capacity | Count lot spaces, plan 50 cars and 100 to 150 people for pilot |
| Insurance | Call the shop's insurance agent first. Event coverage via Hagerty or a rider |
| Permits | City of Fremont: check special event, fire, health (food vendors) rules |
| Budget | Set a monthly cap. Offset with sponsors (parts, tint film, tools) in cash or in kind |

**Run of show (draft, 3 hours)**

| Time | Item |
|------|------|
| 7:30 | Staff arrive, cones and signs, coffee setup |
| 8:00 | Gates open, cars park by category (Euro, JDM, American, Exotic, Trucks) |
| 8:30 | Sim leaderboard opens upstairs (2 racing sims) |
| 9:15 | Raffle one: spin the wheel |
| 9:45 | Sim tournament finals |
| 10:15 | Awards: best build, people's choice, best Still Point-prepped car |
| 10:45 | Closing, raffle two, announce next date |

**Food and drink:** coffee (shop machine or a local cart), pastries or breakfast burritos from a local partner (partner with a Fremont bakery or taquería, gives them a booth and cross-promotes), water. Licensed vendors only for food sales.

**Activities**

- Spin the wheel: prizes from sponsors and EAC (free detail, discounted PPF area, ceramic spray, swag). Keep a prize ledger, value per prize.
- Sim tournament: bracket by lap time on one track and car. Podium prize and a Still Point track credit (TBD).
- Contest ideas: people's choice vote by sticker, best engine bay, best wheel fitment. Avoid public vote counts or social-ranking mechanics per brand rules; use ballots at the event only.
- Prize money: cap at the sponsor budget. Cash prizes carry legal and tax questions. Prefer service credits and gear, and ask an accountant before cash.
- Mini workshops: 10-minute tint, PPF, and ceramic demos at the shop door. They double as content.

**Content from each meet:** a pre-event Short ("here's what's happening"), day-of Reels, one recap long-form, a carousel of attendee cars with permission, and a lead capture QR at registration (name, car, interest).

**Risks to settle before announcing:** liability waiver, noise and burnout rules (no revving, no burnouts, written at the gate), neighbor notice, parking overflow, and weather backup.
