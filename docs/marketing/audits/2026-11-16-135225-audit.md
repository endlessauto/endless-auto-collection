## 11-point checklist on outliers and bottom performers (§7)

**BMW M3 Bushings with PLASTIC (`-8X6nPS`, long-form #1):** Format Pass (DIY demo). Topic Pass (F8X owners search this). Seed **Pass** — "with PLASTIC?!" is the non-obvious hook. Substance Pass (viewer can do the job). Visual/Text/Spoken hooks Pass — 6.84% CTR on 3,101 impressions confirms packaging works. CTA Weak — no next step for a local-service viewer. This is the replicable template.

**Escalade Door Handle Replacement (`oyMqPv`, Shorts #2):** Topic Pass — 7,855 impressions on a Short means real search/browse demand for "2007–2014 Tahoe/Yukon door handle." Text hook Pass (model + years + part = instant clarity). CTA Weak. Searchable, specific, on-target — the Shorts analogue of the M3 template.

**Murciélago — Selling His Childhood Dream Car (`hauU1`):** YouTube served it hard — **7,625 impressions, 1.0% CTR**, the worst click-rate against the most reach on the channel. Text/visual hook **Fail** — the package did not earn the click. Yet it drew **4 subs** (highest on the channel) from the 206 who watched: the topic/story is on-target and engaging, the **packaging is the single brick at fault**. Clear A/B candidate.

**Peaky Blinders Short (`rCftVv`, Shorts bottom, 25 views, 0.43% CTR):** Topic Fail (event, not a buyer concern). Seed Fail (no curiosity gap for a car owner). Off-target — do not remake.

**Shop-revamp long-form series (`xdPBac` 64, `p1QI` 87, `xJcHlo` 112):** High CTR (5.8–7.1%) but tiny impressions — the thumbnail earns clicks from the few served, but the topic is internal/BTS, so YouTube stops serving it. Topic Fail for Game #5; the CTR is wasted on an off-target subject.

## Wrong-audience flag (do not chase)

`apK` Lamborghini STO ride is the highest-view Short (1,789) but tagged `#sema #indecentvehicles`, enthusiast/viral bait, 2 subs, no service relevance. Per Game #5 this is a likely **wrong-audience hit** — a spike in views with no downstream fit. Do not triple down on it.

## One hypothesis, one change to test next batch

**Hypothesis:** On-target, searchable **specific-car + specific-problem how-tos** (M3 bushings, yellow-headlight restore, Escalade door handle, Mustang springs, detail resets) earn both the impressions YouTube rewards and clicks from likely DIY/local buyers; **vlog / event / shop-BTS** content (shop revamp, SEMA vlogs, Peaky Blinders, Cars & Coffee) underperforms on views and skews off-target. Packaging is the biggest lever on already-served videos — Murciélago proves it (7,625 impressions × 1.0% CTR).

**Change (one variable):** Make every long-form a searchable specific-problem how-to and title it `{Year/Car} {Specific Problem} {Fix}`; hold the DIY format constant and change only topic selection toward service/repair jobs with search demand. Pause standalone vlog/event uploads except the sanctioned SEMA brand pilot. Measure CTR and impressions against this batch.

**A/B test (§10, CTR below the 2.81% baseline after 3–4 days):** Re-package Murciélago (`hauU1`, 25 days old, 1.0% CTR) — **new thumbnail only, title held**, 14-day timebox at current traffic. Measure CTR lift from the 1.0% floor. One variable only.

No 10×+ on-target breakout exists in either cohort, so no mandated triple-down set. The suggestions below are pipe-1 remixes of the 2.5–5× near-winners, not a locked formula.
