# Content grader

Score each drafted piece 0–5 on eight criteria (max 40).

| # | Criterion | 5 means | 0 means |
|---|-----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Brand voice | Sentence case, no hype, no exclamation points, active voice | Hype or exclamation points |
| 2 | Hook strength | First line earns the next five seconds; promise stated in 0–15s. Short-form: text, visual, and spoken hooks say the same thing, first sentence is clear, and a true curiosity loop is open | Slow start, throat-clearing, or hook components that contradict each other |
| 3 | Specificity | Real car/build details and numbers | Generic car content |
| 4 | Platform fit | Format matches how Instagram or YouTube is consumed | Wrong length/shape for the platform |
| 5 | Packaging | Title and thumbnail carry one outcome-first promise the video delivers | Mechanism-first, or promise the video can't meet |
| 6 | Proof | Real work or result shown early | Claims without evidence |
| 7 | Next step | CTA is the logical follow-on to what was taught and the asset it points to fits the video's topic (content <> lead magnet fit) and is live or flagged `TBD` | Random pitch, no CTA, or implies an asset that doesn't exist |
| 8 | On-target | Built for owners who could become customers; useful enough to apply | Broad or viral-bait content that attracts the wrong audience |

| Total | Verdict | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 32–40 | PUSH | Show as-is |
| 23–31 | REVISE | Fix the weak part, re-grade, show only the revision |
| Below 23 | REWRITE | Redraft, re-grade, show only the rewrite |

Any criterion scoring 0 caps the verdict at REVISE.
