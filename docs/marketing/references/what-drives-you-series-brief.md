# What Drives You: series brief

Status: draft for sign-off. Premise and community goal come from the founder. Everything under "Expansion" and "Open decisions" is proposed, not locked.

No prior series doc existed in the repo as of 2026-10-20. This is the source of truth.

## One-line answer

What Drives You is a series where car owners tell the story behind their vehicle in their own words. Why they bought it, what it means to them, what they've put into it.

## Premise

Every car at a shop like ours has a reason behind it. A first car, a build that took years, a car someone's dad owned. We usually only see the work. This series gives the owner the mic.

- The owner is the host. We provide the setting, a few questions, and the camera.
- No script, no pitch. Owners share freely.
- The car is the way in. The person is the story.

Pillar: **Build Stories**.

## Why we're doing it

1. **Community.** Car people want to be heard by other car people. We become the place that happens.
2. **Trust.** Under our Game #5 approach, trust comes from relevance and usefulness. Real owners talking about real cars is proof we care about the car and the person, not only the invoice.
3. **Brand fit.** "Driven by Detail" applies to people too. We document the story as carefully as the paint.
4. **Content engine.** Every owner brings their own audience (friends, club, build community). Each episode is a reason for them to share it.
5. **Lead path.** Viewers who see themselves in an owner are likely customers. Metric stays leads per video, not views.

## What it is and isn't

| Is | Isn't |
|----|-------|
| Owner-led, unscripted stories | A customer testimonial or ad |
| Any car, any budget, any story | Only high-end builds or only our clients |
| Specific details: year, model, how they found it | Generic "I love my car" |
| A relaxed conversation | A rapid-fire hype reel |

## Format (starting point)

- **Long-form (SEMA pilot candidate):** 4 to 8 minutes, owner telling the story with the car in frame.
- **Shorts / Reels cutdowns:** the single strongest line or moment, 20 to 45 seconds, hook = the emotion or surprise from the story.
- **Carousel:** the car, three facts, one quote.
- **Build Archive tie-in:** if we did work on the car, link the archive page. Never force it.

Core question set (adapt per owner):
1. What is it, and how did you find it?
2. What made you keep it, or go after it?
3. What's a moment in this car you'd never forget?
4. What have you changed, and why?
5. What does it say about you?
6. What drives you?

Title and thumbnail: one clear emotion, shown through a specific detail, not hype. See the titles and thumbnails feedback memory.

## Expansion: where it can go

Proposed in order of effort. Pick which to commit to.

1. **Open submissions.** A simple form on the site and a DM prompt. Owners who aren't customers can submit a story or a short phone video. Lowers our filming load and widens community.
2. **Repeatable segments.**
   - *The first car.* Everyone has one.
   - *Inherited.* Cars passed down.
   - *The long build.* Years in the making.
   - *Daily driven.* Cars that get used, not trailered.
3. **Owner spotlights on the site.** A What Drives You page collecting episodes, filterable by make. Builds SEO and gives the series a home.
4. **Meet-ups.** Quarterly shop gathering where owners meet and we film on the spot. The series becomes a live community.
5. **Team voices.** Our own crew tells what drives them. Ties to Shop & Team.
6. **Owner-shot episodes.** Owners film themselves with a prompt card. Scales without us on site.

## Guardrails

- Owner consent in writing before anything posts. Owner can pull their episode.
- No pay-for-play, no ranking, no "best story" competitions, no like-count framing.
- Don't edit meaning. Trim for length, never reshape a story.
- Never invent specs or quotes. Anything unknown is `TBD`.
- Sentence case, no exclamation points, no hype.

5. Dedicated site page and submission form now or later?
6. Do we offer owners anything (a detail, a print of the thumbnail) or keep it purely free-share?
