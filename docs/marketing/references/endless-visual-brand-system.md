# Endless visual brand system v1.0

Source: derived with ChatGPT, saved as the starting point. Iterate here. Consumers: thumbnail and graphic work in `publish.md` and `content-engine.md`.

Direction: premium automotive, cinematic, modern, slightly futuristic. Black, metallic, warm gold, high contrast. Confident, refined, technical, energetic. Premium over flashy: when choosing between adding an effect and removing one, remove one.

Avoid: cartoonish graphics, overly colorful designs, generic mechanic-shop look, clutter.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Endless Black | #050505 | Primary background |
| Graphite | #191919 | Panels, shadows |
| Endless Gold | #F5B51B | Signature accent: highlights, borders, key words |
| Highlight Gold | #FFD45A | Bright lit parts of gold |
| Burnt Gold | #C77A00 | Gradients, depth |
| Metallic White | #F4F4F2 | ENDLESS wordmark, key text |
| Steel Silver | #BFC1C3 | Metallic gradients, secondary type |

Ratio: 70% black/charcoal, 20% white/silver, 10% gold. Gold is an accent, never a fill.

## Typography

- ENDLESS: wide, extended, heavy automotive sans, slight forward lean when fitting, white-to-silver metallic finish, thin black outline or shadow. Motorsport feel, not dealership.
- Secondary text (AUTOWORKS, AFTERHOURS, topic names): clean geometric sans, wide letter spacing.
- Afterhours sub-brand: ENDLESS in block font, "Afterhours" in elegant gold script, gold crescent moon, Endless Sphere background.

## Graphic treatments

Metallic lettering with subtle bevel. Gold that reads as light, not yellow paint. Black backgrounds. Fine gold outlines and geometry (wireframes, circles, globe lines). Localized glow only. Depth layers: foreground type, then subject, then subtle background graphic. The gold wireframe globe is the "Endless Sphere" for Autoworks, Afterhours, Events, Presents.

## Effects

Soft gold glow. Subtle metal texture. Tight dark drop shadows. Thin outlines. Vignette fine for thumbnails. Lens flare only at real lights or gold. Motion lines occasionally for motorsport. Gradients: black to charcoal, gold to amber.

## YouTube thumbnail formula

1. Hero subject (car, person, actual component)
2. Short headline, 2-5 words
3. Small Endless branding that never competes with the subject
4. One gold highlight element (arrow, glow, circle, underline, part highlight)

## Photography

Deep blacks, slightly warm highlights, crisp reflections, realistic cars, visible carbon, no oversaturation, faces preserved, darker backgrounds to isolate the subject, gold from the graphic layer and never by recoloring the car.

## Shorts and Reels (9:16)

Top: series branding. Center: 16:9 footage. Bottom: captions and hook. Keep the layout identical across a series.

## Brand architecture

ENDLESS (master), ENDLESS AUTOWORKS (business), ENDLESS AFTERHOURS (casual shop talk), ENDLESS BUILDS (future), ENDLESS EVENTS (car meets, community).
