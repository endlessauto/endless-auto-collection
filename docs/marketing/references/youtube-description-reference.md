Match its structure, order, and sectioning. Where it conflicts with `writing-style.md` (hype words like "incredible", em dashes), the structure wins and the banned words still do not get reproduced in new drafts.

## What this style does

- Opens with the cars and place, names the shop in sentence one, then promises the view.
- Three short paragraphs: what you see, one rarity fact, the mood. Then who it is for.
- Emoji mark section headers only, never bullets inside a section.
- Chapters name each car, with a "(Fan Cam)" style note when a shot differs.
- Featured cars list includes cars that appear but get no chapter.
- Cross-link to one prior video, then the What Drives You pitch, then Connect, About, Music.
- Exactly three hashtags, the first one the most specific car or topic.

## Fixes to apply when drafting from it

- Rarity claims need a source. The Sesto Elemento line hedges (20 planned, reports of 10 built). Keep the hedge unless the user supplies a source.
- The "More from" link must be a real URL with the full title, not a truncated one.
- The Apply link goes to a page that exists in this repo (`/whatdrivesyou`).
- Write "Santana Row, San Jose, California" with a comma, not an em dash, in new drafts.
