Slide 3 — Content
01
Engine bay first.
Lift the hood. If the battery sits near the strut mount bolts, disconnect it.
Slide 3 — Text only.
Slide 4 — Content
02
Loosen the top bolts. Don't remove them.
13mm and E18 E-torx at the top of the mount. They come out last.
Slide 4 — Text only.
Slide 5 — Content
03
Move the ABS sensor. Free the end link.
Hold the end link with a T30 and loosen the nut.
Slide 5 — Text only.
Slide 6 — Content
04
Support the rotor with a jack.
Then remove the strut bracket bolt: 18mm on the front side, 16mm on the back.
Slide 6 — Text only.
Slide 7 — Content
05
Spread the spindle.
Twist the spreader 90 degrees to release the strut slightly. Leave it in to hold it.
Slide 7 — Text only.
Slide 8 — Content
06
Now the strut comes out.
Loosen the top bolts the rest of the way and pull it from the car.
