Ceramic coating on a daily driver changes some things and not others. Gloss holds for years instead of weeks, and washes take less time. Fremont dust and 880 road film still land. Sap and bird droppings still need a rinse, the coating just buys you time. Rock chips are a job for film. On sound paint, expect about 2 to 5 years, with daily freeway cars at the short end.
