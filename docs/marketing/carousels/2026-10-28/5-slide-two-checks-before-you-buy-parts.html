TikTok title: F80 M3 differential bushing check: look and test for play | Fremont, CA
Caption:
Before you replace F80 M3 differential bushings, confirm they are the problem. Two checks: look for cracks or rips, then test for play. Put a crowbar on the rear differential bracket and wiggle the differential. Any play means the bushing is too soft to hold it. Support the car properly first.
