Caption:
Car detailing can't fix everything, and it helps to know which is which before you book. Swirls live in the clear coat, so correction can level them. Rock chips, sun-damaged clear coat, curb rash and torn leather are missing material, and no polish or conditioner brings that back. Our Lexus RX 330 spoiler is the example: sanded, painted, then cut and polished before it looked right.
