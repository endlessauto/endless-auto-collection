S650 Mustang rear lowering springs don't swap like the fronts. The subframe has to come down, and only one side at a time, or it can shift out of alignment with the chassis. Here is the order we followed on our 2024 Mustang GT, from brake line slack to the final torque. Parts, tools and every torque spec are in the guide at endlessautocollection.com/s650-mustang-lowering-springs. Support it, then pull the shock.

03
Lower it until the spring comes out.
Remove the 13mm bracket bolts and 21mm main bolts, then let the jack down slowly.

05
Seat the coil in the isolator groove.
A coil touching metal, or sitting out of the groove, squeaks over bumps.

Hand-thread first, then torque.
Main subframe bolts 129 to 140 lb-ft, brackets 40 to 41, shocks 66 to 70.
