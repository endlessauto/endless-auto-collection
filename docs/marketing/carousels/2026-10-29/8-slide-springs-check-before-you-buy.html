Caption:
Before you order lowering springs, check three things: fitment, the listed drop, and the tools the job needs. On our 2024 Mustang GT (S650 with MagneRide), Steeda lists a 0.75 in front and 0.375 in rear drop on the coupe, and notes it can vary by package and trim. The job also needs a spring compressor and a way to support the rear subframe.
