Caption:
VLT is the visible light a tint lets through. It tells you how dark the glass looks, not how much heat it rejects. Heat is infrared. Ceramic film targets that band, and it avoids the older metallic films that can bother tags and antennas. Fronts stay lighter in California, so we set the pair after you look through the shades.
