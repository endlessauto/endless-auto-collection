Caption:
DA sander vs hand sanding: on our Lexus RX 330 spoiler, the DA won on consistency. By hand, we kept going back for extra sanding to clear marks from earlier passes. The DA was more even and left fewer pigtails, which meant less rework before the cut and polish. The full grit order, 1200 to 3000, is in our guide at endlessautocollection.com/electric-hvlp-spray-gun-diy-car-paint. #dasander #wetsanding #paintcorrection #autopainting #fremontca
