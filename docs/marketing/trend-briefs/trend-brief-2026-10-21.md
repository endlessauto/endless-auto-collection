## Findings

1. **The Escalade how-to Short keeps gaining; a named-model fix is our best repeatable Education format.**
   Source: [youtube-catalog.md](../youtube-catalog.md): 820 views against a Shorts range of 22 to 1,385, up from 752 a day earlier. Earlier briefs (2026-10-16, 2026-10-19) flagged this Short; what changed is the size of the daily gain (+68), which suggests search or suggested traffic rather than a launch-week spike.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: cut the Mustang GT springs install into a one-problem Short with the model and years in the title, the way the Escalade Short is titled, and end on one next step (book an alignment or a consult). Check that the springs Pt.1 footage has a clean 30-second segment.

2. **Detailing trade coverage describes strong 2026 demand for coatings and PPF as add-on revenue for detailers.**
   Source: [Detailing Mafia, car detailing trends in 2026](https://www.thedetailingmafia.com/blog-details/car-detailing-trends-in-2026) and [Professional Carwashing & Detailing, ceramic coatings](https://www.carwash.com/tag/ceramic-coatings/), per search summaries (pages not opened). Both are trade or vendor content with no figures I could verify, so treat as directional.
   Pillar: Shop & Team.
   EAC angle: supports leading with the Sienna-style full detail as the entry service and mentioning ceramic or PPF as the next step, as our best Shorts already do. No numbers to quote.

3. **A PPF brand reworked its line with a 10-year warranty and faster heat self-healing claims.**
   Source: [Detailing Mafia, Automechanika 2026](https://www.thedetailingmafia.com/news/automechanika-2026), per search summary (page not opened; event date not confirmed, and this is a product claim from the vendor, not a test). New since earlier briefs, which only had SEMA 2025 PPF news.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: an owner-facing explainer on what a film warranty covers and what it does not, using the exact warranty terms of the film we install. Do not repeat the self-healing claim without our supplier's documentation.

4. **Shop-video advice stays consistent: simple how-tos and before-and-after shots, but how-to is crowded.**
   Source: [Tomorrow's Technician, how social media videos can set shops apart](https://www.tomorrowstechnician.com/how-social-media-videos-can-set-shops-apart/) and [AutoLeap podcast recap](https://autoleap.com/blog/podcast-preview-episode-21), per search summaries (pages not opened). The AutoLeap recap says Shorts are a lower-cost option for shops and that the how-to category is already competitive.
   Pillar: Process & Craft.
   EAC angle: the crowding argues for specificity (our model, our shop, our numbers) over generic how-tos, which matches what is working for the Escalade Short.
