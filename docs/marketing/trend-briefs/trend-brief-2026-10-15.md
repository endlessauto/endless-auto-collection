## Findings

1. **Detail transformations on ordinary cars are our strongest signal; follow up on it.**
   Source: [youtube-catalog.md](../youtube-catalog.md), Sienna Short at 1,384 views against a catalog Shorts range of 20 to 1,384. Industry write-ups also name correction reveals and transformation footage as the clips that engage on short video ([Your Harlow](https://www.yourharlow.com/2026/03/23/how-this-car-detailing-content-uses-video-to-win-clients/)).
   Pillar: Process & Craft.
   EAC angle: another high-mileage family car, shown start to finish with the specific problems named (stains, odor, paint condition), and one next step at the end.

2. **Longevity proof, such as hydrophobic beading after a year of daily use, is a recurring ceramic format.**
   Source: [TikTok ceramic before and after listing](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/before-and-after-ceramic-coating). A 2026 trade piece says the selling point is shifting toward lower maintenance rather than gloss alone ([Echelon Elite](https://www.echelon-elite.com/post/ceramic-coating-trends-2026-to-watch)). No view counts were available, so this is a format signal only.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: revisit a coated customer car after months of use and show the water behavior and what maintenance it took. Use only real cars and real timelines.

3. **SEMA runs November 3 to 6 at the Las Vegas Convention Center.**
   Source: [The Weekly Driver SEMA 2026 guide](https://theweeklydriver.com/2026/10/sema-show-2026-complete-guide/). Per that guide, Tuesday to Thursday is trade only and Friday, November 6, is open to ticket holders aged 16 and over.
   Pillar: Shop & Team.
   EAC angle: the existing SEMA portfolio ideas brief (2026-10-14) targets this window. The two-week lead time favors the ideas that need the least production, for example the tint percentage comparison.

4. **California front-side tint rules are inconsistently described online, which is a gap an accurate explainer can fill.**
   Source: [Gilroy Blackout 2026 guide](https://gilroyblackout.com/blog/california-window-tint-laws-2026-updated-guide/). Sources agree on a 70% VLT minimum for front side windows (CVC § 26708) but disagree on how aftermarket film is measured against factory glass. They did not agree, so verify against the statute before any script uses a number.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: the tint percentages idea from the 2026-10-14 brief, with a meter reading on a real car and the statute text on screen. Verification is a blocker before drafting.
