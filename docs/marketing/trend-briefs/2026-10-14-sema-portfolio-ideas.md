# SEMA portfolio ideas: education and brand story

Date: 2026-10-14. Goal: build a body of work before SEMA (early November) that shows what Endless does and who is behind it, without reading as an ad. Audience: owners who could book us, plus enthusiasts who will meet us at SEMA.

## Demand check (YouTube autocomplete, live pull 2026-10-14)

- "ceramic coating vs": ppf, wax, graphene, no coating, paint protection film, wrap. "ppf vs": ceramic coating, no ppf, paint, rocks. "car wrap vs": paint, ppf, ceramic coating. The decision comparison is the dominant search shape.
- "paint correction": black car, white car, scratches, before and after, sun damage, and ceramic coating. "swirl marks": black car, removal, after buffing, on ceramic coating, on new car.
- "window tint percentage": comparison, explained, california, 35 percent, 15 percent, 5 percent.
- "how to maintain your car": beginners, paint, battery, engine. Broad, so narrow it to a specific symptom.
- "how we started auto shop": nothing. The origin story won't win search; it wins trust. Treat it as brand content, not a search play.

## Making education not feel promotional

1. **Teach the decision, not the service.** Title and first 30 seconds help someone choose, even if they never book us.
2. **Show the work before any claim.** Real car, real gauge reading, real before and after, on screen in the first minute.
3. **Say what it doesn't do.** Ceramic doesn't stop rock chips. Correction can't fix paint that's too thin. Naming limits is what makes the rest believable.
4. **Include a case where we'd say no or do less.** Rare and specific, never hypothetical.
5. **One quiet next step at the end** that follows from what was taught (tint comparison tool, consultation), never a mid-video pitch.
6. **People talk like people.** Unscripted answers beat read lines. Keep the shop noise.

## Origin story: interview questions (all `needs detail`)

For Will:
- Where did you work and what did you do before Endless? Which part of that still shapes how you run jobs?
- What was the moment you decided to open the shop, and what almost stopped you?
- What did the first month look like? First customer? First mistake?
- Why did you bring [user] on, and what did you ask them to take on?
- When did you know Kevin was the third person? What changed once there were three?
- What is the hardest part of building this from the ground up? What is harder than you expected?
- What does Endless look like in a year? What will you not compromise on?

For the user and Kevin: same structure, from their side (how they met the shop, what they do daily, what surprised them).

Filming note: record it at the shop after hours. This fits the Endless Afterhours sub-brand in `endless-visual-brand-system.md`.
