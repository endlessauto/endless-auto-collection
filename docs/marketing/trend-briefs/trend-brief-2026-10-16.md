## Findings

1. **Model-specific how-to Shorts are our second-best format.**
   Source: [youtube-catalog.md](../youtube-catalog.md): Escalade door handle Short at 691 views against a Shorts range of 22 to 1,385; bushing puller long-form at 270 against a long-form range of 44 to 429.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: a short, named-model fix or check an owner can use before booking, such as how to tell failing tint from dirty glass, ending on one next step.

2. **YouTube reports large demand for satisfying detail restorations.**
   Source: [YouTube culture and trends: auto detailing videos](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/auto-detailing-videos/). Per the search summary (page not fetched), detailing videos with "ASMR" in the title drew over 150 million views in the first half of 2026. The most-cited example, The Detail Geek, uses very dirty vehicles with a full before-and-after. Those are long-form; no reliable Shorts-specific data was found.
   Pillar: Process & Craft.
   EAC angle: a longer cut of the Sienna-style detail with natural shop sound and minimal voiceover, with the before-and-after at the start. Verify the 150 million figure on the page before quoting it.

3. **Color PPF was the main PPF story at SEMA 2025; no SEMA 2026 coverage is out yet.**
   Source: [XPEL SEMA 2025 release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251103528913/en/XPEL-Debuts-COLOR-PPF-Ultra-Exclusive-Pagani-Imola-Roadster-Live-Demos-at-SEMA-2025) and [Window Film, SEMA 2025 PPF recap](https://wf.mydigitalpublication.com/articles/sema-2025-vegas-glitz-for-ppf). New since the 2026-10-15 brief: we now know what last year's show centered on, which suggests color PPF versus vinyl wrap will be a live question again. This is an inference, not a report.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: a plain comparison of color PPF and vinyl wrap (protection, cost range, removal) with a real car if one is in the shop. Time it against the SEMA window (Nov 3 to 6).

4. **Forum chatter questions whether "ceramic" coatings are truly ceramic.**
   Source: [Team-BHP thread, "Why do most new detailing studios struggle"](https://www.team-bhp.com/forum/modifications-accessories/312422-why-do-most-new-detailing-studios-struggle-2.html), dated Sept 25, 2026 per search. Commenters say some products sold as ceramic are silicon dioxide suspended in polymers. A single forum thread, so a weak signal.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: a transparent "what is in the coating we use and what it does" explainer, consistent with documentation over hard sell. Needs the exact product data sheet; do not state chemistry from this thread.
