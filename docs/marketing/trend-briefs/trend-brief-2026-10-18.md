## Findings

1. **A "guess the color" reveal on a heavily dirty luxury car is the breakout pattern in one detailing channel analysis.**
   Source: [OutlierKit analysis of Revamper M4](https://outlierkit.com/channel/revamperm4). Per the search summary (page not opened), the pattern is a mud-caked recognizable car whose dirt hides the paint color, then a reveal. The same analysis suggests quick before-and-after Shorts and warns of formula fatigue when title and thumbnail patterns repeat. It rates replicability medium. This is a single-channel case study, not a market trend. New since earlier briefs: those covered satisfying restorations in general; this adds the specific reveal mechanism.
   Pillar: Process & Craft.
   EAC angle: our Sienna Short already works on a before-and-after. The same structure could hold the hook (paint or interior condition hidden until the reveal) on an ordinary customer car, with the problems named and one next step at the end. Needs a real car with enough visible dirt; do not stage one.
