## Findings

1. **California tint rules are a steady search topic, and shop guides disagree on specifics.**
   Source: [UPPF, California window tint laws 2026](https://www.uppf-usa.com/blogs/post/california-window-tint-laws-2026) and [Rapid Window Tinting](https://www.rapidwindowtinting.com/post/california-window-tint-laws-2026-what-s-legal-what-you-need-to-know). Guides agree on the broad rules: front side windows need 70% light transmission combined with the glass, rear windows have no limit with two outside mirrors, and the windshield allows a strip at the top. They disagree on fines ($25 base plus assessments in one, a $250 fix-it citation in another) and on whether the 88% figure applies to the film alone. The statute is California Vehicle Code 26708; check it before quoting. No change in the law was found. This is new since earlier briefs, which did not cover tint.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: a plain "which tint percentage fits your car" explainer that matches the tint.html comparison tool, with the rules confirmed against the statute and what we actually install. Fits the playbook's teach-then-offer-the-tool conversion path.
