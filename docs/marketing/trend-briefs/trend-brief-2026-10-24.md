## Findings

1. **The owner-story Short is still gaining on day two, which makes the Build Stories hook a better candidate than the meet vlog format.**
   Source: [youtube-catalog.md](../youtube-catalog.md): 988 views, +76 against 912 yesterday, while the Escalade Short gained only +8. Yesterday's brief called one day too early to judge; what changed is that day two held up. Still no watch-time or lead data.
   Pillar: Build Stories.
   EAC angle: the owner's own words as a question title is the pattern. For the next Build Archive car, pull one line from the owner interview as the Short's title, and end on one next step. Check Studio for watch time and any inquiries before treating it as repeatable.

2. **Car meet vlogs are the weakest long-form performers so far.**
   Source: [youtube-catalog.md](../youtube-catalog.md): Santana Row 64 views and Cars and Coffee 63, against 440 for the Sienna detail and 220 for the springs install. Two videos is a small sample, and the Santana Row video is one day old, so this is a flag, not a conclusion.
   Pillar: Shop & Team.
   EAC angle: if meet footage is shot again, tie it to a customer car or a service the shop did, rather than posting it standalone.

3. **A public outlier ranking for automotive Shorts exists, but it is dominated by brand and dealership campaigns.**
   Source: [ytubeinfluencers, top automotive outlier Shorts](https://ytubeinfluencers.com/outliers/automotive/shorts/), per search summary (page not opened). Search found no 2026 outlier list specific to detailing Shorts. New since earlier briefs, which pointed to 1of10 as the outlier tool.
   Pillar: Education.
   EAC angle: not usable for daily research. For the two-week mining pull, filter to detailing, PPF and tint shops with 10K to 1M followers and skip brand campaigns.
