# Shoot script: five Shorts, one session (2026-11-16)

For Will. Read it, then say it your way. Everything below is built so you never have to think about what to say, only how.

## How to use this

- **LOCK** = say it close to word for word. These are the hook and the facts. They come from our site, so they have to stay true.
- **BEND** = yours. Each BEND line has two or three alternates. Pick one, mash them together, or say it however it comes out.
- Everything in *(italics)* is delivery, not words.
- Say the hook line first on every take. It has to match the text on screen.
- Shoot every scene three ways: read it straight, then faster, then your own words. Keep the best.
- Say "Endless" or "Endless Auto Collection" on camera. Don't say a handle. The IG name is changing, so we use "link in bio" and the site.
- No exclamation points in the captions. Your spoken energy can be as big as you like.

**Next step on every Short:** request a consultation. The form is on every page of endlessautocollection.com (the Consult button). In the caption: "Request a consultation, link in bio."

### Answer bank: film these back to back (10 minutes)

All answers come from our service pages. Each is 10 to 20 seconds. Same setup as S2: Will to camera, same car behind him, the question as the opener. Put a different shirt on between every three takes and it looks like a series.

1. **"Can I take my PPF through a car wash?"**
   "I'd skip the automatic one. The brushes can still scratch the film, even though the film is protecting the paint under it. *(LOCK)* Hand wash it. Or come see us." *(BEND)*

2. **"Do I still have to wash a ceramic-coated car?"**
   "Yes. *(beat)* Ceramic makes washing shorter. It doesn't replace it. *(LOCK)* Think of it as a nonstick pan, not a self-cleaning pan." *(BEND)*

3. **"How long does ceramic last?"**
   "On good paint, about two to five years of real water beading. *(LOCK)* Garage cars last longer. If you drive 880 every day, you're at the short end. *(LOCK)* Anyone who says forever is selling you forever." *(BEND)*

4. **"Does ceramic stop rock chips?"**
   "No. It's not armor. That's PPF. *(LOCK)* Ceramic is how the paint, or the film, stays easy to live with." *(BEND)*

5. **"Front-end PPF or full body?"**
   "Front end covers the chip zone: bumper, hood, fenders, mirrors, headlights. Full body covers the painted surfaces around the car. *(LOCK)* If your car lives on 880, start at the front." *(LOCK: from the site's FAQ.)*

6. **"Does PPF hide chips I already have?"**
   "No. Film follows the surface under it, so chips and flaws still show. *(LOCK)* The prep is what decides how good it looks. More prep time, better install." *(LOCK)*

7. **"Does PPF only come in clear?"**
   "No. It comes in color. Change the color and protect the paint with one film." *(LOCK)* *(BEND: "Yes, I'll wrap you in protection. Don't say it like that.")*

8. **"My headlights are yellow. Can I put film on them?"**
   "Restore them first. Then the film goes over a clear lens. *(LOCK)* Film over yellow is just protected yellow." *(BEND)*

9. **"Do I need an appointment?"**
   "Yes. We're by appointment only at our shop in Fremont. *(LOCK)* No walk-ins, and honestly, that's how you get my full attention." *(BEND)*

10. **"How much is PPF?"**
    "We quote it as a package after a consultation. It depends on the car and how much of it you want covered. *(LOCK)* We don't list a price on the site. Request a consultation, link in bio. *(LOCK)*"
