# Keyword research: SEMA 2026 after-hours vlog (2026-11-13)

Pulled live on 2026-11-13 with `scripts/yt_keyword_research.py` (YouTube autocomplete, A to Z expansion on 26 seeds, then the top 10 results and 20 newest uploads for 38 candidate keywords). Re-run it for every video.

## What these numbers are and are not

- They are public-data proxies: autocomplete (what people type), top-10 view counts and dates (how strong the competition is), and how many of the 20 newest uploads landed in the last 7 days (how fast the topic is moving).
- They are not Ahrefs keyword difficulty or monthly search volume. No free source gives those for YouTube. If you have vidIQ, TubeBuddy or Ahrefs, paste the numbers for the shortlist below and they go into this table.
- Results shift between pulls (same keyword, hours apart, the medians moved 5 to 60 percent), and vary by location. Shorts are excluded. Treat the labels as bands, not exact values.

## Fact checks

1. **"SEMA Content Creators Club" is unverified.** No public listing found (two searches, one extended). A different Las Vegas group, Saucey's Creator Club, runs karaoke nights and is unrelated to SEMA. Official SEMA after-hours events this year included the Industry Honors on Nov 5 at Fontainebleau and a Friday afterparty at Zouk. Do not put "Content Creators Club" or "SEMA" ahead of the event name in a title until you confirm the organizer and the exact name. "After SEMA" is accurate either way.
2. **"Sema" alone is polluted.** On YouTube it returns Turkish, Indonesian, Tamil and Fijian music, plus a person's name. Always pair it with "2026", "Las Vegas" or a car term.
3. **"Karaoke" in a Vegas title attracts song-track searchers.** "vegas karaoke" autocompletes to backing tracks (Doja Cat, Elvis, Waking Up in Vegas). The real-venue phrases ("karaoke bar las vegas", "karaoke in las vegas") are small and mostly three to four years old. Keep "karaoke" as a supporting word, not the lead keyword.
4. **SEMA 2026 dates:** Nov 3 to 6, Las Vegas. The videos in the results confirm uploads from the show are still landing daily.
5. **Ramen claims:** "las vegas ramen challenge" is the number one suggestion for "vegas ramen". Only use "challenge" if the ramen was an actual challenge on camera.

## What the data says

- **Nothing is both low competition and high volume for this video.** The honest closest matches are the SEMA terms with a lens: sema 2026 party, car creators vegas, and (for another video) sema 2026 detailing.
- **The window is closing.** 14 of the 20 newest "sema 2026" uploads came in the last 7 days. SEMA ended Nov 6. Publish in the next 24 to 48 hours.
- **Karaoke is a reason to click, not a search term.** Put it in the title as the stake and the thumbnail, not as the keyword.
- **Winning pattern in this week's SEMA vlog titles:** a named action plus SEMA 2026. "We Took Over SEMA 2026! Here's What Happened..." got 34,758 in three days. "Roasting Cars at SEMA Until We Get Kicked Out" got 10,801 in seven hours. Large-channel titles use hype words, which this brand does not. Keep the structure (an action, a stake), drop the hype.
- **Content gap for EAC.** "sema 2026 detailing" is an exact autocomplete suggestion with zero 100k videos in the top 10 and no EAC video yet. It is on-target for a detailing shop. Logged in the idea inbox.
