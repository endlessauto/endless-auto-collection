# Shopmonkey message templates

Customer-facing canned messages for Endless Auto Collection. Fremont, CA · (510) 709-7097 · endlessautocollection@gmail.com

## How to use

- **SMS** = short, one text. **Email** = longer, with subject line.
- Placeholders in `[brackets]`. Map to Shopmonkey merge fields when you load each one: `[first name]`, `[vehicle]` (year make model), `[service]`, `[estimate total]`, `[date]`, `[time]`, `[advisor]`, `[link]`.
- `TBD:` marks a shop policy I don't have (hours, deposit, cure times, warranty terms, payment methods). Fill these once, then they're set for every template.
- Voice: precise, transparent, no hype. Sentence case, active voice, no exclamation points. Documentation over hard sell.
- Sign every message with the advisor's first name, then "Endless Auto Collection".
- Send photo and video updates inside the Shopmonkey thread so the history stays with the work order.

## 10. Service-specific pre-visit prep

Send with the appointment confirmation (3.1).

**PPF**
Wash the car before drop-off if you can. Tell us about prior chip repair, touch-up paint, or aftermarket coating. Plan for [TBD] days without the car.

**Ceramic**
Tell us about any prior wax, sealant, or coating. We assess paint on arrival; correction depth is scoped after we see the car. Plan for [TBD] days.

**Tint**
Remove dash cams and anything on the glass. Tell us which windows and what shade. California front-side limits apply; we'll confirm what's legal before we cut film.

**Wrap**
Bring any color reference you have. Note existing dents, rock chips, or clear coat failure; wraps hide color, not damage. Plan for [TBD] days.

**Detailing**
Remove personal items and car seats. Tell us about stains, pets, smoke, or odor. Choose exterior, interior, or full detail.

**Mechanical (maintenance, inspection, diagnostic)**
Describe the symptom: sound, when it happens, warning lights. Bring service records if you have them.
