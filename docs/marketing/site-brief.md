## Voice

Source: `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `docs/marketing/references/writing-style.md`. Precise, transparent, documentation over hard sell. Sentence case, active voice, no exclamation points, no em dashes.

| Say | Don't say |
|-----|-----------|
| The film, coating, or part by name | "Premium", "world-class", "best in the Bay" |
| Hours, price ranges, coverage areas, what is not covered | "Don't miss out", "limited spots" |
| "We" for the shop, "Will" for the owner | "Passion for", "elevate", "seamless" |
| The specific car and job | "Are you tired of…", "In this video, we…" |

Author for how-to and bio blocks: Will Xu, owner (surname from `about.html`) ⚠️ NEEDS INPUT: credentials, years in the trade.
