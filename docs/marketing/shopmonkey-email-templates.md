# Shopmonkey email templates

Full-length emails for Endless Auto Collection. Companion to `shopmonkey-message-templates.md` (SMS). Use email when the customer needs something to keep: a quote, a confirmation, care instructions, a record.

## How to use

- Placeholders in `[brackets]`: `[first name]`, `[vehicle]`, `[service]`, `[estimate total]`, `[date]`, `[time]`, `[advisor]`, `[link]`. Map to Shopmonkey merge fields when loading.
- `TBD:` marks a shop policy not yet defined. Fill once, reuse everywhere.
- Subject lines: sentence case, under 50 characters, name the car or the action.
- Voice: precise, transparent, no hype. Active voice. No exclamation points.
- Every email ends with the standard signature below. Add the PS line only where marked.
