The site brief names the niche: one Fremont shop that protects, repairs, and prepares European and Japanese performance cars, with documented work and price transparency. The grid doesn't say that yet. Proof of work plus plain explanations of what drives a quote is the lane competitors (Premier, Garage43, DoubleTake) don't appear to use, per the 2026-11-16 strategy doc. Their socials are unverified.
