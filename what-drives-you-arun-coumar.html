Arun Coumar: selling his childhood dream car.

Arun dreamed of a Lamborghini Murciélago as a kid. After 20 months of owning one, he is selling it. He also owns five Driven Auto Care locations across the Bay Area. We sat down with him to ask why he is selling.

Published: Oct 21, 2026
OWNER
Arun Coumar
CAR
2008 Lamborghini Murciélago LP640 coupe
OWNED
20 months
BUSINESS
5 Driven Auto Care locations, Bay Area

Origin story

This Murciélago was registered new in Union City, where Arun was living at the time. Years later, he brought the car back to where it first ventured.

The same day it appeared on Facebook Marketplace, Arun bought a flight across the country. He flew out to meet an Italian grocery store owner at his mansion, and there he test drove his dream car in the snow.

That is how the car found its way home. Twenty months on, Arun is selling it, and the video is about why.

What we asked

Five questions, in this order, filmed on two cameras. Then we kept talking.

  1. 1

    Arun, if this was your childhood dream car, why are you selling it?

  2. 2

    What does owning this car represent to the younger version of you, and what would the younger version of you say if he knew you were standing here today to sell it?

  3. 3

    Do you think having a dream car can actually change the way someone operates in life?

  4. 4

    Do you think the car made you successful, or did becoming the person who could afford the car make you successful?

  5. 5

    Does selling it feel like you're losing something, or does it feel like you're making room for something bigger?

After the five questions, the conversation went freestyle: how long Arun owned the car, whether it cost him money or appreciated, and what comes next.
