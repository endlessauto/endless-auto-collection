FAQ

How do you price a job?

Quoted as a package after consult. Labor rate is not listed on the site.

Do you do mechanical work?

Yes — maintenance servicing, inspection, and diagnostic. See the mechanical services page for what's covered.

Do you tune or modify?

Not as a public lead offer. Ask at consult.

What films do you use?

Premium only. Wraps use 3M and Avery Dennison. PPF packages use self-healing film with a 10-year manufacturer warranty.

Do you document the install?

Yes. Professional BTS photo and video of the process is part of how we work — Driven by Detail, not just a slogan.

What services do you offer in Fremont?

Paint protection film, ceramic coating, window tint, custom wraps, car detailing, and mechanical services. See each service page for what is covered.

Where is Endless Auto Collection and do I need an appointment?

We are at 41907 Albrae St, Fremont, CA 94538, by appointment only. Call (510) 709-7097 or request a consultation.

What areas do you serve?

Fremont, plus Newark, Union City, Milpitas, Hayward, the East Bay, and Silicon Valley.
