# Viral blueprints

Four repeatable short-form formats the team wants to make our own. Use them when asked for viral ideas, tests, or "what could we film" around a shoot or event. Each one is a test: log comments, saves, shares and leads per video, keep what brings leads, drop the rest. EAC plays Game #5, so never propose a blueprint for views alone. Every piece ends on a real next step.

**Read first:** `references/writing-style.md`, `content-engine.md` (hook rules: text, visual, spoken say the same thing).

## The four blueprints

| Blueprint | Why it works | EAC version |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Product reveal** | Sensory payoff | Tease the car (cover, shadow, a hand on the paint, cold start), hard cut into action. Cinematic, pure SFX, no voice. Example: AMG GT R tease straight into the pit exit |
| **My reaction if [moment]** | Fuels comment debates ("How would you react?") | One face, one relatable moment, 7 to 10 s. Range from funny to raw to relatable. Pull from real track-day, detailing or shop moments. Never stage a fake emotion |
| **Is it possible [thing] @ [place or account]** | Builds a high-stakes curiosity loop | Open the loop in the first second, pay it off in the same clip. Example: Is it possible to drive this with no track experience @ Thunderhill. Use "from $X" with a price Stillpoint has confirmed, never "as low as" |
| **Giving strangers unsolicited advice** | Playful pattern interrupt | Needs a script and cast. Make it read as a skit unless the person agrees on camera afterward. Name the cast, get a yes from each |

## Rules

- Tag exactly one pillar per idea (Build Stories, Process & Craft, Shop & Team, Education).
- Facts come from `docs/marketing/site-brief.md` or the user. Partner prices, horsepower and cast are `TBD` until confirmed. Never invent a reaction, quote or customer.
- No hype, no FOMO, no exclamation points, no em dashes. "My reaction if" is the on-screen label, not a drama ellipsis.
- Not affiliated with BMW or Mercedes-Benz. Say AMG GT R, imply no endorsement.
- Ask before filming anyone and get a name. No plates, no phone numbers.

## Where they live
