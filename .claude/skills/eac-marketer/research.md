# Research

Web search for content that helps EAC. Read `docs/marketing/references/youtube-engine-playbook.md` section 1 and `docs/marketing/references/kallaway-short-form-playbook.md` sections 2–3 and 6 first.

## Scope

- Trending car-build, detailing, PPF, ceramic, tint content formats
- Competitor shop activity
- Automotive culture moments worth tapping into
- Follow-ups on notes in `docs/marketing/content-calendar.md` marked as performing well. Real performance data outranks generic trend findings when they conflict.

## Method

Find outliers: videos far above their own channel's average, not just high view counts. Note why the format worked so the psychology can be reused with a different mechanism. Check outliers' channels for other winning formats.

## Idea mining (every 2 weeks)

Follow the Kallaway mining process: 10–15 competitor accounts, last 30 days, outlier score = views ÷ that channel's average (mark `est.` if computed by hand), find the 5–7 recurring topics, extract the non-obvious seed of each winner, shortlist 10–20 `topic + seed` ideas with the source link. Spend 80%+ of effort on competitor short-form outliers. Skip anything not relevant to EAC's buyer (owners who could book PPF, ceramic, tint, wrap, or detail work). For each shortlisted idea note which bricks to keep (format, hook, structure) and which 1–3 to change.

## First-reps sprint

When asked for first videos: find 5 competitor accounts (10K–1M followers, 1–2 stages ahead), then 3 outliers (5×+ their channel average, est. if by hand) across 3 different formats with low production friction for EAC. For each: link, format, why it worked, hook breakdown, and the proposed remix (hold format, hook structure, script flow, visual style; change the idea). Hand to `content-engine.md`.

## Output

3–5 bullets. Each: finding, source link, pillar tag, and one concrete angle EAC could take. No generic filler; if a finding has no EAC angle, drop it.

## Modes

- **On-demand:** answer in chat. Save only if asked.
- **Scheduled (daily):** first run `python3 scripts/refresh_youtube_catalog.py` to update `docs/marketing/youtube-catalog.md`. Then write `docs/marketing/trend-briefs/trend-brief-YYYY-MM-DD.md` and notify. Research only. Never draft content, touch the calendar, or post.
