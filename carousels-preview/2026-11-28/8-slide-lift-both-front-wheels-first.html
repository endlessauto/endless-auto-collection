Caption:
S650 Mustang lowering springs start at the front, and the front has an order. Lift both front wheels before you touch the sway bar end links, or the bar is preloaded and the nut will not come off. Then hang the caliper, thread the knuckle nuts flush before the hammer, and plan on new knuckle bolts. They are stretch fasteners.
