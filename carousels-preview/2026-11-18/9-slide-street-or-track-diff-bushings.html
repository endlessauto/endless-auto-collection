Caption:
F80 M3 diff bushings come in two polyurethane hardnesses, and it's a choice you feel every day. On the Revshift dual mount kit from our 2018 M3 job, 80A street (black) is slightly stiffer than factory rubber with no added NVH. 95A track (green) is significantly stiffer, with a minor increase in noise, vibration and harshness. This car commutes daily, so the miles you actually drive should pick the hardness, not the spec sheet.
