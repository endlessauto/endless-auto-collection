A DIY bushing puller can do the same job as the kit: push the old F80 M3 differential bushing out into a cup wider than the bushing. We built ours from hardware-store parts and used it on a 2018 M3. Three pieces: a cup, a long bolt, and a back piece the size of the bushing. Parts list and steps in the guide: endlessautocollection.com/f80-m3-differential-bushing.
