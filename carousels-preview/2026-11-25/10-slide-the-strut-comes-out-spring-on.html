Caption:
F80 M3 front strut removal doesn't need a spring compressor. The compressor is for taking the spring off the strut, and for building the new one. On our 2018 M3 F80 Bilstein B16 job: top bolts loosened first, the bottom freed, a spindle spreader twisted 90°, and the strut came out. On another day we used the lift for clearance and pulled a front strut with the spring still on. On this job the spring came off with the compressor fully seated, face clear of the top.
