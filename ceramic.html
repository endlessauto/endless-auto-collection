FREMONT · CERAMIC COATING

Ceramic coating in Fremont.

A bonded SiO2 layer that holds gloss, sheds water, and makes weekly washes shorter. Not armor — that is PPF. Coating is how the paint or film stays easy to live with in East Bay dust and sun.

How long it lasts

Why people coat the car

Gloss that does not rinse off in a month

Wax is a weekend product. A professional ceramic is a multi-year surface.

Washes take less time

Hydrophobic surface means dirt has less to grab. Fremont dust and 880 film still show up — they just leave easier.

UV and chemical noise

Sun, bird bombs, and tree sap. Coating buys you time to rinse instead of etching overnight.

It finishes PPF

Film stops chips. Coating on top is how water beads. Most PPF jobs get this stack.

DURABILITY

How long ceramic coating lasts

Honest range for a professionally installed coating on sound paint: about 2 to 5 years of real hydrophobic behavior. Garage-kept cars last longer. Daily 880 drivers sit at the short end.
