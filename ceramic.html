Ceramic coating in Fremont.

A bonded SiO2 layer that holds gloss, sheds water, and makes weekly washes shorter. Not armor — that is PPF. Coating is how the paint or film stays easy to live with in East Bay dust and sun.

How long it lasts

Why people coat the car

Gloss that does not rinse off in a month

Wax is a weekend product. A professional ceramic is a multi-year surface.

Washes take less time

Hydrophobic surface means dirt has less to grab. Fremont dust and 880 film still show up — they just leave easier.

UV and chemical noise

Sun, bird bombs, and tree sap. Coating buys you time to rinse instead of etching overnight.

It finishes PPF

Film stops chips. Coating on top is how water beads. Most PPF jobs get this stack.

Ceramic FAQ

How much does ceramic coating cost in Fremont?

Ceramic coating starts at $900 for a coupe, $1,000 for a sedan, and $1,200 for an SUV, with paint correction included. If your paint is already corrected, coating-only is by quote. We do not coat paint that needs correction, and we confirm the final price after we inspect the car.

How long does ceramic last here?

Our ceramic coating is a 5 year coating. On sound paint, expect about 5 years of real hydrophobic behavior. Garage-kept cars last longer, daily 880 drivers see more wear, and we reassess instead of promising forever.

Do I need PPF too?

Ceramic is not chip armor. If freeway grit is the worry, film the impact zones first, then coat. Many Fremont jobs are that stack.

Is correction included?

Yes. Paint correction is included in our ceramic coating price, and it comes before the coating. We do not coat paint that needs correction. If your paint is already corrected, ask about coating only, which we quote separately.

Do you document the coating?

Yes. BTS photo and video of prep and application is part of how we work.

Can ceramic coating protect restored headlights?

It is one of the options we compare for keeping a restored lens clear, next to clear film. Read our headlight restoration guide for how restoration and protection fit together.
