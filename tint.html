Tint FAQ

What is legal on the front sides in California?

Front-side VLT is tightly limited. 70% is the typical legal front-side target we discuss. Rear shades can go darker. Final shade is confirmed at consult — the slider is a preview, not a CHP meter.

Why ceramic instead of dyed film?

Ceramic targets infrared heat and UV without a metal stack that can bother tags and antennas. Heat on a Fremont lot is the usual reason people upgrade.

Which shade do most cars leave with?

It depends on the glass and the goal. 33% is a common rear look. Fronts stay lighter to stay legal. We set the pair after you look through the shades.

Do you document the tint?

Yes. BTS photo and video of the install is part of the process.
